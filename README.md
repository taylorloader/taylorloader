## How I approach projects

I look at a website as more than a visual design. It needs to communicate the right information, make it easy for visitors to take the next step, represent the property accurately, and support discoverability in search.

My process combines:
1. Understanding the business, audience, and goals.
2. Organizing information into a clear site structure.
3. Designing and building pages around usability and brand consistency.
4. Writing and optimizing useful, search-aware content.
5. Applying technical enhancements and reviewing the live experience.
6. Maintaining and improving the site over time.
